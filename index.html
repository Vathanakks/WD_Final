FAQ

Formula 1 is the highest level of international racing for single seater cars. Drivers compete in races around the world to earn points toward the championship.

The winner of a race gets 25 points, second place 18, third place 15, and so on. Points decide the driver and team standings. You can see point system on your "Result page.

Race schedules are published before the season starts. You can check them on the official Formula 1 site or your “Schedules” page.
